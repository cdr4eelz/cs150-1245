*** Handoffs ***


*** Tradeoffs ***

-Software-
* Convenience​
  - Existing Company-X codebase/libraries​
  - Rapid & familiar development "flow"​
* Optimization Impacts​
  - Non-intuitive/fragile code tricks​
  - Distract from primary focus​
  - Complicate validation tests​
* Timeslicing to address Latency​
  - Inherently complex​
  - Overhead (context switching)​
  - Difficult to guarantee (non RTOS)​

-Hardware-
* Performance​
  - Dramatic boosts possible​
  - CPU offload/parallelism ​
* Design/Test Effort​
  - Offload to perf/parallel specialists​
  - Modular separation possible​



Reaching limits of Software-only design...​
  Brute-force effort (increase CPU speed):​
    Capped by part cost, cascades to support hardware & revalidation, power consumption​
  Multi-core CPU:​
    Shared bottlenecks remain: memory/cache/bus​
    Cache contention/fouling almost inevitable​
    Generalized CPU cores not optimized for our specific bottlenecks​
​
Key bottlenecks or impediments:​
    CPU wasted on trivial or unsuited activities​
    Needless fouling of I-cache & D-cache​
    Memory & cache contention​
​
Target approach:​
    Establish convenient border between CPU & coprocessor.​
    ​
    Separate (write-only) GPU memory!​
